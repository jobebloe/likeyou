About us

GemTale sits at the intersection of emotional consumer tech and playful accessories. A friendly ghost figure (3D-printed, bright colors) carries a gem on its belly — the physical token for a cloud adventure log. Pair once via USB + Bluetooth when you receive it; then log moods, notes, and photos anytime. Forward the ghost to a friend: your chapter freezes, theirs begins, and you can follow the traveling gem's story as it moves from person to person.

Vision

We believe objects should carry human experience — not precious metal, but stories that travel. A ghost passed from friend to friend, each adding a chapter with photos and AI-crafted entries. The gem on its belly is the thread. Our vision: the first companion whose story you can forward and follow — one chapter per owner, preserved forever.
